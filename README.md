# Flip Clock

A calm, distraction-free flip clock and countdown timer that runs in any browser. One HTML file, nothing to install.

## Features

**Clock**
- Shows your device's current time in 12-hour format, with hours, minutes and seconds on flipping cards and a small AM/PM card.
- Seconds can be switched off for a quieter look.

**Countdown timer**
- Set hours, minutes and seconds, or tap a preset (10 min, 25 min, 30 min, 1 hour).
- The cards flip down second by second. Start, pause, resume and reset at any time.
- Only the cards you need are shown, and they grow to fill the space: hours, minutes and seconds for an hour or more, just minutes and seconds under an hour, and only seconds under a minute.
- When time is up, the screen flashes black and white, a beep plays and phones vibrate. Tap anywhere to stop it (it also stops on its own after a minute).
- The countdown keeps running if you switch back to the clock.

**Look and feel**
- Two themes: **Classic** (dark, retro) and **Clean** (light, easy on the eyes in daylight). It follows your device's light or dark setting until you pick one.
- Soft, colourful doodles in the background (frangipani, roses, pencils, books, drones, robots, a deer and a hippo), scattered differently every time. Adjust their opacity with a slider or reshuffle them.
- A clean screen: tap anywhere to show or hide the controls.
- Fullscreen button (or press `F` on a keyboard).
- Fits any screen: cards sit side by side in landscape and stack in rows on portrait phones and iPads.
- Keeps the screen awake while it's open, on browsers that support it.
- Remembers your theme, seconds setting and doodle opacity.
- Works offline. The only online request is the Oswald font from Google Fonts, and it falls back to a system font without a connection.

## Download

1. Open `flip-clock.html` in this repository.
2. Click the **Download raw file** button at the top right of the file view.
3. Save it anywhere on your device.

Prefer everything at once? Use **Code → Download ZIP** on the repository page.

## Run it on your devices

| Device | How to run it |
| --- | --- |
| Windows, Mac, Linux, Chromebook | Double-click `flip-clock.html`. It opens in your default browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari). |
| Android | Open the downloaded file with Chrome from your Files or Downloads app. |
| iPhone and iPad | Safari can't open a downloaded HTML file directly, so open it from a web link instead (see below). Then tap **Share → Add to Home Screen** for a full-screen view with no browser bars. |

Good to know:
- The Fullscreen button isn't supported on iPhone Safari, so use Add to Home Screen there.
- Keep-awake needs a recent browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari 16.4 and later). If your screen still dims, set Auto-Lock or Sleep to Never in your device settings.

### Host it with GitHub Pages (best for iPhone and iPad)

1. In your repository, go to **Settings → Pages**.
2. Under **Source**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, pick `main` and `/ (root)`, then **Save**.
3. After a minute, your clock is live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repository-name>/flip-clock.html`.
4. Open that link on any device and use **Add to Home Screen** to keep it like an app.
